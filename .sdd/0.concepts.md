this is a project that is going to be the guide for a japan trip.

im going to spend a fews days in tokyo with my family. we will departure from São Paulo GRu in October 10th 2026, doing a connection in DOHA. 

We will get back to São Paulo in October 31th 2026.

My idea about this project is to be used as a guide, about useful information, like:

- The curency — how easily convert ien to reais, to know the price when buying things.
- The countdown — days until the trip, or until the departure.
- the hotels: listing the location and places we will be staying.
- the itinerary: every day will be a few places to go and an on going plan, i want to separate the itinerary in days, i want to be able to fill the itinerary and display to them easily.
- useful vocabulary: create a section that is helpful to them when they want to say something to a japanese, and they dont know how to say, like: hi, hello, excuse me, what is that? etc. remember that they speak portuguese and we should use that language as base, and options to emit pre-made phrase japanese.
- Practical info: currency, plugs/voltage, emergency numbers, local transport, tipping norms, language phrases. The boring stuff people actually need.
- Phrasebook — key local phrases, offline, maybe with pronunciation.
- Emergency card — nearest hospital, embassy, local emergency number, an "I need help" phrase — one tap away.

my siblings dont know how to use internet very well, so focus in a easy interface, direct, and minimal.
